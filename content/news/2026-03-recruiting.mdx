Yu Lab welcomes undergraduate researchers throughout the year. Relevant backgrounds include:

- Chemistry, chemical engineering, or physics
- Computer science, with an interest in scientific computing
- Materials science or related fields

Students at any experience level in molecular simulation, Python, or machine learning are welcome. Send Prof. Yu (jiwoongs1492@ajou.ac.kr) a short introduction and describe what you would like to learn or investigate. We will discuss a suitable starting project when we meet.
