The Multiscale Molecular Computational Chemistry Lab (Yu Lab) opened at Ajou University in March 2026 and is jointly affiliated with the School of Frontier Sciences and the Department of Chemistry.

We develop computational methods that connect molecular motion with observable properties in liquids and soft materials. The work combines molecular simulation, machine learning, and statistical analysis, with the level of detail chosen for the question at hand. Current topics include aqueous chemistry, polymer-directed self-assembly, glass dynamics, colloidal transport, and hydrogels.

The lab is led by Prof. Ji Woong Yu, previously a research fellow at the Korea Institute for Advanced Study. We welcome students from chemistry, chemical engineering, physics, materials science, and computer science. See the Contact page for current openings and application details.
