## Abstract

Scaling theory gives the free-energy cost of inserting a particle into a semidilute polymer solution and the depletion attraction between such particles. A particle smaller than the polymer correlation length interacts with chain segments of its own size, while a larger one interacts with whole correlation blobs, and this distinction sets how both quantities scale. Self-consistent field calculations give the thickness of the polymer-depleted layer around cylinders, and the analysis extends to polymer brushes with mobile grafting points.
