## Abstract

A machine-learning potential trained on quantum calculations maps acetic-acid oxidation in high-temperature, high-pressure supercritical water. Compared with a conventional reactive force field, it better reproduces the measured products, radical pathways, and complete oxidation; the conventional model gives the overall reaction barrier closer to experiment.
