## Abstract

Cyclic dimers of aryl vinyl ketones open their rings during radical polymerization, giving vinyl polymers whose units join head-to-head. Density functional theory (DFT) calculations show that ring opening beats the competing ring-retaining addition mainly through activation entropy. Copolymerizing the dimers with ordinary vinyl ketones tunes the head-to-tail fraction, which shifts the glass transition temperature and the photodegradation rate.
