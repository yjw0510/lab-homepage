## Abstract

Two pyrene photosensitizers that differ only in the electron acceptor, benzothiazole or pyridinium, are compared. Density functional theory (DFT) and time-dependent DFT calculations show that pyridinium stabilizes the lowest unoccupied molecular orbital and red-shifts absorption, while benzothiazole strengthens spin–orbit coupling and roughly doubles the calculated rate of crossing to the triplet state. Formulated into nanoparticles, the benzothiazole dye generates superoxide-type reactive oxygen species under 750 nm two-photon light and selectively kills cancer cells.
