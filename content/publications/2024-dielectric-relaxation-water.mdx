## Abstract

Molecular dynamics simulations, which follow molecular motion over time, compare a neural-network model trained on quantum calculations with two conventional pairwise models of liquid water. The trained model better reproduces water's frequency-dependent electrical response, and it alone resolves a high-frequency signal from hydrogen-bond stretching.
