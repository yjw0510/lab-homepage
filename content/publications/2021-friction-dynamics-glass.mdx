## Abstract

Friction time series from driven-probe simulations of a model glass separate rattling in cages of neighboring atoms from slower structural rearrangement. Below a critical drag speed, friction stops fluctuating around a steady value. That speed times the slow-regime duration gives a length growing on cooling.
