## Abstract

Calculations that treat a polymer brush, a dense layer of surface-grafted chains, as a smooth concentration field compute the polymer-depleted zones around two embedded cylinders. Zones beside and above the cylinders compete to set whether the cylinders attract or repel, as molecular simulations confirm.
