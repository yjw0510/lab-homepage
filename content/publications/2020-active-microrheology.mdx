## Abstract

Active microrheology reads local mechanics from the force on a dragged probe. Simulations of a model metallic glass show a threshold force emerging on cooling, below which the probe stays trapped, and friction relaxing in two steps: rattling among neighbors, then slower structural rearrangement.
